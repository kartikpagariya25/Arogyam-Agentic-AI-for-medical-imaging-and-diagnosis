Common medical imaging descriptors:
- Hyperdense: high attenuation, appears white on CT.
- Hypodense: low attenuation, appears dark on CT.
- Hyperechoic: strong reflection on ultrasound, appears bright.
- Hypoechoic: weak reflection, appears dark.
- Homogeneous: uniform texture; heterogeneous: mixed texture.
- Lesion: localized abnormal tissue area.
- Margin: edge of a lesion; smooth, spiculated, or irregular.
- Enhancement: increased brightness after contrast due to blood flow.

Terminology is used consistently across radiology reports to describe structure and pathology accurately.
