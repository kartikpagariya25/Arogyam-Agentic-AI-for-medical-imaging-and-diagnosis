Psoriasis management follows a stepwise approach based on severity and body surface area.

Mild psoriasis:
- Topical corticosteroids, vitamin D analogs (calcipotriol).
- Coal tar, salicylic acid, and emollients to reduce scaling.

Moderate to severe psoriasis:
- Phototherapy (narrowband UVB).
- Systemic therapy: methotrexate, cyclosporine, acitretin.
- Biologics: TNF-alpha inhibitors (etanercept), IL-17 inhibitors (secukinumab).

Lifestyle:
- Stress reduction, smoking cessation, healthy weight.
- Avoid triggers such as infection, certain drugs (beta-blockers, lithium).

Diagnosis considerations:
- Differentiate from seborrheic dermatitis and eczema.
- Histopathology helps confirm uncertain cases.
